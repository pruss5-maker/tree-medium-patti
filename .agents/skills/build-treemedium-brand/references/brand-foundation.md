fork, receives and prepares its message, and then reviews the reading with the client in a private

## Positioning

Use this as the working positioning statement until the user revises it:

> Tree Medium Patti visits a living tree specifically for each client, receives its message through Kela, and shares grounded spiritual guidance to help the client reflect, protect their inner space, and find a way forward.

Use this as the working signature line:

> Your mind should be a sanctuary, not a battlefield.

The Tree Medium concept is the memorable center of the brand. Patti is a warm spiritual guide who treats trees as active participants rather than scenery or generic nature symbolism.

## Desired Audience Experience

Help visitors feel:

- welcomed rather than judged
- grounded rather than overwhelmed
- curious rather than pressured
- protected and capable rather than fearful or dependent
- clear about what the service is and what it is not

## Voice

- Warm and sisterly
- Earthy and grounded
- Mystical but understandable
- Reassuring and protective
- Lightly playful and comfortable with “the woo”
- Direct enough to inspire trust and action

Avoid cold corporate language, fear-based selling, vague spiritual filler, inflated grandeur, and certainty about unverifiable outcomes.
