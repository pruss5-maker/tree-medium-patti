# Plant Oracle research notes

The card copy is original KELA reflection inspired by plant history, symbolism, folklore, and ecology. These themes are not presented as universal spiritual truth or medical guidance.

| Plant | Oracle theme | Research anchor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yarrow | Sacred Boundaries | Historic associations with healing and threshold protection ([UC floriography](https://ucanr.edu/sites/mglaketahoe/files/273526.pdf)) |
| Lavender | Peaceful Clearing | Its name's washing association and its later symbolism of resistance and empowerment ([Kew](https://www.kew.org/plants/english-lavender), [NYBG](https://www.nybg.org/planttalk/shades-of-lavender-symbolism-scent-sightings-and-species/)) |
| Hibiscus | Radiant Presence | Cultural use and symbolism around ceremonies and warding away bad omens ([Brooklyn Botanic Garden](https://www.bbg.org/article/heavenly_hibiscus)) |
| Comfrey | Grow Together | The name's association with “growing together” and a long history of healing use ([University of Wisconsin](https://corn.aae.wisc.edu/Crops/Comfrey.aspx)) |
| Chamomile | Gentle Resilience | Traditional symbolism of patience in adversity |
| Calendula | Returning Light | Longstanding solar, joy, hope, and protective symbolism |
| Broadleaf Plantain | Grounded Repair | Cross-cultural use and its ability to thrive under disturbance ([University of Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-minnesota/yard-and-garden-problems/plantain)) |
| Sunflower | Turn Toward Light | Sun symbolism, deep history of cultivation, and mature eastward orientation ([Kew](https://www.kew.org/plants/sunflower)) |
| Rosemary | Remember Yourself | Remembrance and fidelity traditions ([Australian Anzac Portal](https://anzacportal.dva.gov.au/commemoration/symbols/rosemary)) |
| Garden Sage | Wise Discernment | Long folk association with wisdom and good fortune ([Horniman Museum](https://www.horniman.ac.uk/story/plants-food-and-folklore/)) |
| Mugwort | Dream Threshold | Historic healing-garden and dream-threshold folklore ([RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/digital-collections/healing-garden)) |
| Rose | Open Heart, Clear Thorns | Love, secrecy, and silence in floriography ([The Met](https://www.metmuseum.org/de/essays/rose-floriography-valentine)) |
| Dandelion | Unapologetic Hope | Resilience, transformation, and letting go ([Trinity College Dublin](https://www.tcd.ie/media/tcd/nursing-midwifery/pdfs/Seeds_of_Change-final-online-report.pdf)) |
| Stinging Nettle | Protective Fire | Its sting protects the plant while the plant shelters and feeds other beings ([Natural History Museum](https://www.nhm.ac.uk/discover/stinging-nettles.html)) |
| Peppermint | Fresh Current | Mint's long hospitality and renewal symbolism |
| Basil | Blessed Abundance | Historic aromatic, ritual, and protective use ([University of Haifa](https://cris.haifa.ac.il/en/publications/myrtle-basil-rosemary-and-three-lobed-sage-as-ritual-plants-in-th/)) |
| Echinacea | Enduring Strength | Long cultural and medicinal history, interpreted here without claiming a specific living tradition ([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12808356/)) |
| Jasmine | Heavenly Joy | Renaissance symbolism of divine love and heavenly happiness ([The Met Cloisters](https://blog.metmuseum.org/cloistersgardens/2008/09/19/jasmine-goes-west/)) |
| Lemon Balm | Heart's Return | Historic associations with gladness, restoration, and bees ([Herb Society of America](https://blog.herbsociety.org/herb-of-the-month-lemon-balm-medicine-for-the-plague-and-the-blues/)) |
| Red Clover | Reciprocal Plenty | A nitrogen-fixing plant that enriches soil and supports pollinators ([Kew](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A523575-1/general-information)) |

## Artwork provenance

The 20 botanical portraits were generated with OpenAI image generation in default mode, then optimized to 720×1080 WebP. The shared prompt requested a botanically recognizable single plant, vertical 2:3 composition, naturalistic botanical photography with a restrained vintage-plate texture, woodland-garden atmosphere, accurate plant colors, no text, no border, and no watermark.
