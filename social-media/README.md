# KELA social-media concept

## The idea

Each post feels like a card finding the viewer—not an advertisement interrupting them. The animal
or tree is the visual focus, Patti's language remains calm and direct, and the invitation is always
to notice what appears rather than to promise a prediction.

The system uses the oracle artwork already on the site. Card colors remain a replaceable accent so
the layouts and motion can be completed while the final palette is still being tuned.

## Core formats

- **Feed post:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Best for Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest.
- **Story/Reel:** 1080 × 1920 (9:16). Keep essential words away from the top and bottom interface
  areas.
- **Website link preview:** 1200 × 630. Continue using the existing Open Graph images in
  `assets/social/`.

The `samples/` folder contains feed and vertical masters for Blue Owl and Madrona.

## Motion concept

Use one restrained 7–9 second loop:

1. The empty card rests in soft light for one second.
2. The animal or tree eases into view with one living detail: a blink, breath, feather shift, leaf
   movement, light change, or subtle camera drift.
3. The name and short phrase appear: for example, “Blue Owl — Quiet confirmation.”
4. The closing frame reads “Take your free daily draw” with the short KELA URL.
5. Hold the closing frame long enough to read, then return to the opening without a hard cut.

Movement should feel observed in nature, never cartoonish. Respect reduced-motion settings on the
website; social exports can also have a still version.

## Repeatable weekly series

- **Animal Calling:** one animal, its keyword, and one short sentence from its public oracle text.
- **Tree Calling:** one tree, its keyword, and one short sentence from its public oracle text.
- **Dream State:** one approved Patti phrase from the private draft queue, published only after
  human review.
- **Behind the Scenes:** Patti, a living tree, sketch details, or the making of the oracle. Never use
  material from a private client reading.

A simple starting rhythm is three posts per week: Animal Calling, Tree Calling, and either Dream
State or Behind the Scenes.

## Caption pattern

> **[NAME] — [KEYWORD]**
>
> [ONE SHORT ORACLE SENTENCE]
>
> What were you thinking about when this found you?
>
> Take your free daily draw at treemedium.vercel.app/[animal-oracle or tree-oracle]

Use a small, consistent hashtag set rather than a long block. Suggested starting set:
`#KELAOracle #AnimalOracle` or `#KELAOracle #TreeOracle`, plus the specific animal or tree name.

## Guardrails

- Never post subscriber addresses, private-reading details, private video links, or identifying
  client information.
- Keep psychic protection language attached to the individual animal or tree shown.
- Describe cards as reflection, guidance, or confirmation—not guaranteed prediction.
- Use Patti's exact phrase for Dream State material and require review before publishing.
- Keep the complete reading on the website. Social posts share only a short opening line and invite
  the viewer to draw their own card.

## Color handoff

The accent color may change the card outline, keyword, and one soft glow. The cream background,
forest-green typography, spacing, artwork scale, and CTA stay fixed. This keeps every post visibly
KELA while allowing card colors to be tested without rebuilding the system.
