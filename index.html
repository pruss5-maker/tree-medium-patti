Why receive a Tree Message?

Your mind was never meant to be a battlefield.

Negative thought patterns are the Goliath that KELA, the collective voice of the trees, is here to help us face. Through a private Tree Message, Patti enters spiritual listening with one living tree and shares the guidance that arrives for you. The reading can become a protected pause: a place to breathe, reflect, and notice what no longer deserves so much room.

When the constant noise begins to loosen, the space it leaves behind can feel unfamiliar. It may first feel empty, even boring. But boredom is not the end. It is open ground. From that quiet, positive thoughts, new ideas, resolutions, and goals can begin to surface. The mind starts to feel more like a sanctuary and less like a battlefield. There is finally room to breathe and then room to create.
