Protection invocation

Your mind deserves to be a sanctuary.

Cover your eyes and close your mind. Tune into the guide that found you and its energy. Each animal, tree, or plant offers one suggested spiritual meaning. Hold that quality as a clear place for your attention to return. In KELA’s spiritual language, each time you tune in meditatively to a tree, plant, animal, or any living presence you love, you are speaking life back into your field.

When you feel its presence, ask out loud and in your mind. Each card addresses the guide by name. Tree Oracle cards include the word “Tree,” such as “Dear Olive Tree.” Speak: “Dear [guide], I can feel your presence within me. Please be the guardian of my mind. Please protect my thoughts and energy. Only allow kind and encouraging thoughts to enter my field. Amen.”
