Messages from the Trees

Meet KELA.

KELA is the name the trees gave Patti for their collective voice—many trees speaking together across the Earth and beyond.

One day, as Patti walked into her apartment surrounded by trees, they asked her to call their collective voice KELA. The name came before the research. When Patti looked it up afterward, she found the Hebrew word קֶלַע (KELA): a sling or slingshot. The related Hebrew root also carries a sense of braiding or weaving—an echo of many tree voices joined together with purpose.

Messages from KELA

Past. Present. Future.

Patti’s personal tree photographs hold three messages from KELA—what we carry from our source, what stands with us now, and the direction in which we choose to grow.

How KELA came through

A living conversation.

In 2024, Patti’s ability to receive and communicate messages with trees came into focus. The trees describe themselves as the slingshot that can help take down the Goliath of humanity’s repetitive thoughts that no longer serve us.

KELA has shown Patti unkind thoughts as bugs gathering around the mind and energy field. In Patti’s spiritual practice, the trees work with the plant and animal kingdoms to offer mechanisms for psychic protection and help ease the mind and energy.

These are Patti’s spiritual impressions and symbolic reflections, offered for personal contemplation.
