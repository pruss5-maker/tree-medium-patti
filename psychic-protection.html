A KELA teaching from Tree Medium Patti

Your mind is sacred ground.

Psychic protection is the practice of choosing what receives your attention, what is welcome in your inner world, and what no longer has access to you.

Why protection matters

Ease the mind of the collective

Protection begins with returning to yourself.

The trees showed Patti how much outside noise, repetition, collective pressure, and other people’s expectations can disturb the mind and energetic field. They asked her to help ease the mind of the collective.

KELA’s response is not fear. It is discernment. Not every thought deserves your belief. Not every feeling requires action. Not every person needs continued access to your attention. Protection creates enough quiet to recognize what is truly yours.

Protection in ordinary life

Boundaries change the energy you live inside.

01

Guard your attention.

Psychic protection can be as simple as noticing a thought that pulls you away from yourself and deciding not to keep feeding it. You do not have to wrestle with every thought. You can breathe, return to your body, and choose a thought that supports the life you are building.

02

Goodbye is protection too.

Saying goodbye to an ex—or blocking them when continued access keeps reopening the connection—is not necessarily punishment. It can be a boundary that ends the cycle of checking, waiting, rereading, and being pulled back into an old version of your life.

03

Choose the frequency you feed.

Shifting your frequency does not mean denying grief or pretending everything is positive. It means choosing thoughts, habits, spaces, and relationships that align with the person you are becoming—and with the love you deserve to receive.

The closed door can be loving

Blocking access makes room for a new alignment.

A relationship can end while its rhythm continues in the mind. A name on the screen, a late-night message, or the habit of looking can reactivate the same hope, hurt, and uncertainty.

Closing that access gives your nervous system and spiritual life room to settle. You are not erasing what happened. You are protecting the space where your next self—and your next relationship—will grow.

You do not have to remain available to what keeps you unavailable for your own life.

A meditative KELA practice

Ask a living guide to help protect your mind.

Choose a favorite tree, plant, or animal—one whose presence already helps you feel steady. You do not need to force a vision. The practice is simply a way to gather your attention around a trusted living presence.

  1. 1
    Arrive. Put both feet on the ground and take three slow breaths.
  2. 2
    Tune in. Picture your tree, plant, or animal. Remember its shape, movement, texture, or sound.
  3. 3
    Ask. “Protect my mind and energy. Help me release thoughts that do not serve me. Help me return to what is mine.”
  4. 4
    Receive. Rest quietly. Notice what softens, what becomes clearer, and what boundary asks to be honored.
  5. 5
    Close. Thank your guide, feel your feet again, and take one grounded action that protects your peace.

A protection request

Beloved tree, plant, or animal,
stand with me now.

Protect my mind and energy.
Quiet what does not serve me.
Strengthen what is true in me.

Help me close what is complete,
choose what is aligned,
and return to my own sacred ground.

Thank you. I am here. I am listening.

The KELA way

Protection is not a wall around your life.

It is a clear edge around your peace. It lets love move without giving every thought, message, person, or pressure the right to enter. You can be openhearted and protected at the same time.
