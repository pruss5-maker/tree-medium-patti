Privacy Notice

Effective September 30, 2026

Lumen Pleroma LLC offers KELA: Messages from the Trees through Tree Medium Patti. This notice explains what personal information we receive, why we use it, and the services that help us provide Tree Medium reading and coaching.

Information we receive

Depending on how you contact or book with us, we may receive:

  • Your first name and the first initial of your last name, entered in the booking window.
  • Your phone number, only if you ask for a phone call after you review the reading.
  • The email address you enter on the Stripe payment page.
  • Purchase status, amount, transaction identifiers, and limited payment details from Stripe.
  • Messages you send to Patti and information needed to prepare or deliver your reading.
  • The private YouTube link for your recorded reading, and the time for your phone call.

Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe. We do not receive or store your complete card number on this website.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Process and confirm your purchase.
  • Schedule, prepare, and deliver your Tree Medium reading and coaching session.
  • Communicate about questions, cancellations, refunds, or service updates.
  • Prevent misuse, maintain security, and keep necessary tax, accounting, and business records.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and protect legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use third-party services to complete the booking and reading experience. Those providers process information under their own notices and, where applicable, on our behalf:

  • Stripe for secure payment processing.
  • FormSubmit to email Patti the name and phone number from the booking window.
  • YouTube/Google for the private recorded reading emailed to you.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent fraud or harm, or protect our rights and the rights of others.

Recordings and sensitive information

The reading is a private recorded YouTube video, about 5–7 minutes, emailed as a link within 1–3 business days. Please do not share that link. A follow-up phone call happens only when you request one. That call is live and is not automatically recorded. If a call will be recorded, Patti will provide notice first.

Please avoid sending government identification numbers, account passwords, complete payment-card numbers, medical records, or other information that is not needed for your reading.

Retention and protection

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the service, maintain required business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information, but no online service or storage method can promise absolute security.

Free Oracles

The Animal, Tree, and Plant Oracles select a guide inside your browser and save that day’s result in your browser’s local storage. The site also remembers the name and image of each guide selected that day so your round companion symbols can travel with you from page to page. The oracles do not require an account and do not send your selected guide or a private question to Lumen Pleroma LLC. You may clear the saved results through your browser settings.

When you complete a memory game, the site also saves the Animal, Tree, or Plant card that found you in this browser. It remains in your companion circles until another card from that same memory game replaces it. This memory stays in your browser and is not sent to Lumen Pleroma LLC.

Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics on public pages to understand aggregate traffic such as page visits, referring sites, approximate country, device type, browser, and operating system. Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies and stores anonymized data for aggregate statistics rather than identifying individual visitors.

We do not use this information for advertising. The private Patti Studio is excluded, and the analytics do not receive your selected Oracle guide, a private question, or anything you type into Stripe or another booking or payment service. Microsoft Clarity is not currently active on this website.

Learn more in Vercel's Web Analytics privacy and compliance documentation.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held directly by Lumen Pleroma LLC. Some information may need to be retained for transactions, legal obligations, security, or recordkeeping. Requests involving Stripe, FormSubmit, or Google may also need to be directed to that provider.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the service or our privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, email treemedium@proton.me.
