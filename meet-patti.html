Meet your Tree Medium

Hi, I’m Patti.

I listen to trees—not as scenery or symbols, but as living spiritual presences with something to say.

In 2024, I tuned into my skills when my husband pointed out my unique ability to receive and communicate messages with the trees. His recognition helped me trust what had been asking for my attention.

My path has always centered care: I served for three years as a licensed massage therapist and for eight years as a newborn hearing screening coordinator. Today, I serve Spirit as a Tree Medium, helping people protect their minds with mechanisms KELA stands by.

For each private reading, I visit one living tree for one person, enter Spirit Mode, listen for the words, images, and impressions that arrive, and prepare the message to share live with you.

This is a reflective spiritual practice, offered with care and without promises of fixed predictions or guaranteed outcomes.
