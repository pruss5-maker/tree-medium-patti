Privacy Notice

Effective August 23, 2026

Lumen Pleroma LLC offers KELA — Messages from the Trees through Tree Medium Patti. This notice explains what personal information we receive, why we use it, and the services that help us provide Your Tree Message.

Information we receive

Depending on how you contact or book with us, we may receive:

  • Your name, email address, and other contact information you provide.
  • Your scheduling details and any question or intention you voluntarily share.
  • Purchase status, amount, transaction identifiers, and limited payment details from Stripe.
  • Messages you send to Patti and information needed to prepare or deliver your reading.
  • Zoom meeting details and a recording of Patti’s findings when private recorded delivery is needed.

Payment-card information is entered directly into Stripe. We do not receive or store your complete card number on this website.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Process and confirm your purchase.
  • Schedule, prepare, and deliver Your Tree Message.
  • Communicate about questions, cancellations, refunds, or service updates.
  • Prevent misuse, maintain security, and keep necessary tax, accounting, and business records.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and protect legal rights.

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use third-party services to complete the booking and reading experience. Those providers process information under their own notices and, where applicable, on our behalf:

  • Stripe for secure payment processing.
  • Calendly for scheduling.
  • Zoom for the live video meeting.
  • YouTube/Google when Patti provides findings through a private video link.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal process, prevent fraud or harm, or protect our rights and the rights of others.

Recordings and sensitive information

Live client conversations are not automatically recorded. If a live session will be recorded, Patti will provide notice first. When a client cannot attend within the non-refundable period, Patti may create a recording of her own findings and deliver it through a private YouTube link. Please do not share that link.

Please avoid sending government identification numbers, account passwords, complete payment-card numbers, medical records, or other information that is not needed for your reading.

Retention and protection

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the service, maintain required business records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information, but no online service or storage method can promise absolute security.

Free Oracles

The Animal, Tree, and Plant Oracles select a guide inside your browser and save that day’s result in your browser’s local storage. The site also remembers the name and image of each guide selected that day so your round companion symbols can travel with you from page to page. The oracles do not require an account and do not send your selected guide or a private question to Lumen Pleroma LLC. You may clear the saved results through your browser settings.

Optional website analytics

If you choose “Allow analytics,” we use Microsoft Clarity to understand aggregate interaction patterns such as page visits, clicks, scrolling, attention, device and browser type, and technical errors. Clarity may reconstruct a session from page content and interaction events so we can identify confusing navigation, dead ends, or sections that may be too long. It does not create a camera or screen video of you.

Optional analytics are off until you affirmatively allow them. We deny advertising storage, do not use the information for advertising, and do not record anything you type into booking or payment services. Stripe and Calendly open outside this website and are not part of KELA's Clarity observation. You may change your choice through the “Privacy choices” control in the footer.

Learn more in the Clarity data documentation and Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information held directly by Lumen Pleroma LLC. Some information may need to be retained for transactions, legal obligations, security, or recordkeeping. Requests involving Stripe, Calendly, Zoom, or Google may also need to be directed to that provider.

Changes and contact

We may update this notice as the service or our privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. For privacy questions or requests, email treemedium@proton.me.
