Meet your Tree Medium

Hi, I’m Patti.

I listen to trees as living spiritual presences with something to say, not as scenery or symbols.

In 2024, I tuned into my skills when my husband pointed out my unique ability to receive and communicate messages with the trees. His recognition helped me trust what had been asking for my attention.

My path has always centered care: I served for three years as a licensed massage therapist and for eight years as a newborn hearing screening coordinator. Today, I serve Spirit as a Tree Medium, helping people protect their minds with mechanisms KELA stands by.

For each private reading, I visit one living tree for one person, enter Spirit Mode, listen for the words, images, and impressions that arrive, and prepare the message to share live with you.

This is a reflective spiritual practice, offered with care and without promises of fixed predictions or guaranteed outcomes.

Why I listen

Why I do this work.

KELA has shown me that negative thought patterns can become a Goliath inside the mind, taking up the space where peace, ideas, and possibility want to live. The trees do not ask us to fight those thoughts forever. They help us quiet the noise, create psychic protection, and return the mind to sanctuary.

When that noise leaves, the first feeling may not be inspiration. It may be emptiness. It may even be boredom. I see that quiet as sacred space. After boredom, we begin to create. We imagine again. We discover new thoughts, resolutions, and goals.

This is why I listen to the trees: to help create room inside you to breathe, receive what is meant for you, and meet what wants to come next.
