KELA · Messages from the Trees

Many trees. One collective presence.

Kela is the name the trees collectively gave to Patti—not one tree, but roots, trunks, branches, and voices woven together across the Earth and beyond.

The name carries an echo of weaving, sacred boundaries, and purposeful direction. For Patti, Kela feels collective, protective, and alive.

How KELA came through

Four messages at the roots.

01

The slingshot

Leverage for a Goliath.

The trees describe themselves as the slingshot—not the stone—helping us face the Goliath of humanity’s repetitive thoughts that no longer serve us.

02

Thoughts as bugs

See what is circling.

KELA has shown Patti unkind, repetitive human thoughts as bugs gathering around the mind and energy field—an image for noticing what asks to be cleared.

03

Collective care

Protection from the kingdoms.

In Patti’s spiritual practice, the trees work with the plant and animal kingdoms to share mechanisms for psychic protection and to help ease the mind and energy fields.

04

The year I listened

My ability came into focus.

In 2024, my husband pointed out my unique ability to receive and communicate messages with trees. His recognition helped me tune into the skill that had been asking for my attention.

These are Patti’s spiritual impressions and symbolic reflections, offered for personal contemplation.
